# Drum samples used by `djembe/render.py`

All files are CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication), verified on each Freesound page in October 2026. CC0 needs no attribution; the authors are credited here anyway. The files are Freesound's public high-quality MP3 previews (128 kbps, 44.1 kHz), not the original WAVs. None of them comes from the IEMP corpus.
